02

M.Sc. dissertation

M.Sc. Physics · Pachaiyappa’s College · University of Madras · 2022

Supervisor: P. Catherine

Growth and comparative study of morphology of cadmium nitrate p-nitrophenol single crystals

My M.Sc. dissertation explored the growth and morphology of cadmium nitrate p-nitrophenol (CNPN) single crystals using the slow evaporation solution method. I studied the grown crystals through single crystal XRD, FTIR and UV visible spectroscopy. This work introduced me to crystal growth, material characterization and the relationship between morphology and optical properties.

Laser shock peening of metals and alloys

I gained introductory exposure to laser shock peening (LSP), with a focus on its use to improve wear resistance in metals and alloys, particularly 3D printed metals. This experience introduced me to surface treatment and the connection between material processing and mechanical performance.
