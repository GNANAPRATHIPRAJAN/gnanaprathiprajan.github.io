Workshop · Quantum science & computation

Attending “Physics Insights: Quantum Mechanics to Computation” gave me a fresh perspective on how quantum ideas connect with computation. As a physics student, I was inspired by the scope of quantum science in this new era and its potential to shape how we explore and solve problems. The workshop strengthened my curiosity about the path from fundamental quantum mechanics to computational applications.

My NPTEL Deep Learning Exam Experience

Read moreRead less

On 17 October 2026, I took the NPTEL Deep Learning exam for the course offered by IIT Ropar.

I found the questions challenging. Some needed more thought than I expected, and I had to slow down and work through them carefully. I managed to attempt the exam by thinking through what I had learned, even when the answers were not immediately clear.

The experience reminded me that learning a subject also means being able to use it when a question feels unfamiliar. I still have more to learn, but working through a difficult exam was an important part of that journey.
